# Course schedule

**Nextflow in Action: Build Smarter, Faster, Reproducible Pipelines**

The course runs for **2 days** (Wednesday 18<sup>th</sup> to Thursday 19<sup>th</sup> November 2026), with classes from **13:00 to 17:00** (Swiss local time, GMT+1) each day. Every day is composed of interleaved lectures and hands-on exercises, including a 15-minute break every day. All live sessions are recorded and will be made available at its due time on this course website.
